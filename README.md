## Research

**Oil Supply Shocks and Cross-Border Dollar Credit: Evidence from China**
with Ahmed Ahmed (Babson College) and Oyakhilome Wallace Ibhagui (IFC and Johns Hopkins) · *Under review, Energy Economics*
Supply-driven oil-price increases raise foreign banks' dollar claims on Chinese banks; the response is concentrated in bank claims and reverses in later quarters.
`Oil supply shocks` `BIS locational banking statistics`

**What Drives Currency Hedging in US Multi-Asset Funds? Risk, Cost, or Asset Composition**
*Working paper, 2026*
Using SEC Form N-PORT filings, I measure funds' foreign bond and equity holdings and FX forward positions by currency. Net forward sales track foreign bond holdings more than equity holdings, the opposite of a minimum-variance benchmark, suggesting asset composition drives hedging more than risk-based benchmarks imply.
`SEC N-PORT` `FX forwards` `Minimum-variance hedging`

**Do Data Centers Affect Nearby Housing Values?**
with Lei Zhang (Old Dominion University) and Tammy Leonard (UT Southwestern Medical Center) · *In progress* · Southern Economic Association, November 2026
Estimates the effect of data-center openings on single-family house prices, testing distance decay, exposure intensity and pre-opening anticipation.
`MLS transactions` `Staggered difference-in-differences`

**[International Heterogeneity in Equity Factor Exposures](https://github.com/Tonyshrewd/International-Heterogeneity-in-Equity-Factor-Exposures)**
with Pavel A. Solopov and Maria Sultana (Old Dominion University), supervised by Mohammad Najand · *Early stage*
Do equity factor themes (value, momentum, low risk, …) move with national stock markets the same way across countries? Uses the Jensen–Kelly–Pedersen global factor data for 95 countries, with country-level regressions and Shapley decompositions of explanatory power.
`R` `JKP Global Factor Data`

## Projects

**[Social Media Attention and Stock Volatility](https://github.com/Erdos-Projects/fall-2025-financial-markets-and-sentiment)**
with Morgan Bryant, Nihan Akis Man and Francis Westhead · Data Science Boot Camp, Erdős Institute, Fall 2025
Do spikes in social-media attention forecast realized volatility and large next-day returns? Daily Bluesky post counts and returns for 50 Fortune 500 firms, modeled with GARCH-X, random forests and gradient boosting.
`Python` `GARCH-X` `XGBoost`

**[How Valuable Is Financial Flexibility When Revenue Stops? A Replication](https://github.com/Tonyshrewd/financial-flexibility-covid19-replication)**
Full replication of Fahlenbrach, Rageth & Stulz (2021, *RFS*) on cash, debt and firm value during the COVID-19 crash, with an extended sample.
`R` `Compustat/WRDS`

**[Directional Tolling and Crash Risk on I-64](https://github.com/Tonyshrewd/i64-tolling-policy-and-crash-risk)**
Difference-in-differences study of VDOT's 2018 dynamic tolling on I-64 in Hampton Roads: no change in morning crashes, a significant rise in evening crashes near ramps and merges.
`R` `Difference-in-differences`

**[California AB 413 "Daylighting" and Pedestrian Safety](https://github.com/Tonyshrewd/ab413-daylighting-crash-panel)**
City-month crash panel (2019–2026) to test whether enforcing no-parking zones near crosswalks reduced pedestrian and cyclist injuries, using a staggered difference-in-differences design.
`R` `Staggered DiD`
